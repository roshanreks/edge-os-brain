# The 5 Levels of Ecom Scaling (EDGE OS)

Revenue bands are **monthly** (Indian Lacs / Crore).

| Level | Monthly Revenue     | Primary Job                          | Creative Testing Volume (guideline) | Ad Spend % of Revenue |
|-------|---------------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| 1     | 0 – 5 Lacs         | Find real PMF + first edge           | 8–20 concepts/month                | 30–50%               |
| 2     | 5 – 20 Lacs        | Double down on what works            | 20–50                              | 25–35%               |
| 3     | 20 – 50 Lacs       | Raise creative volume + cheaper traffic + new customers | 50–120                    | 20–28%               |
| 4     | 50 Lacs – 1 Cr     | Systems + associations + retention   | 100–250+                           | 15–22%               |
| 5     | 1 Cr+              | Full edge domination + experiments   | 200–500+                           | 12–18%               |

These bands come from the original handwritten roadmap and have been calibrated against 2025–2026 Indian D2C data (CAC inflation, creative fatigue, the common 1–50 Cr ARR wall, retention as the real P&L lever).

Moving up a level requires simultaneous progress on:
- Unit economics (especially repeat rate and contribution margin)
- Creative velocity and sophistication
- Fulfillment reliability
- Awareness expansion (moving beyond Product/Most Aware audiences)
