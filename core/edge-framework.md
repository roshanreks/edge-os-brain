# EDGE Framework

**EDGE** = the real 80/20 that decides winners in e-commerce.

The five components:

1. **Product** — Does it solve a real problem better or differently? Is there genuine product-market fit (repeat rate is the truth serum)?
2. **Branding** — Positioning, visual identity, voice, emotional territory.
3. **Creative** — The ads, content, and storytelling that actually convert attention into demand.
4. **Association** — Influencers, celebrities, brand partnerships, cultural affiliations that transfer trust and awareness.
5. **Distribution / Awareness** — How more people come to know about you (paid, organic, PR, offline, community).

**Core operating rule** (from the original map):

> Once you have something working → Find out / extract your edge. Make more of what is working.

Everything else (targeting, media buying tricks, fancy funnels) is secondary. Without a clear EDGE that is being deliberately multiplied, scaling becomes expensive and fragile.
