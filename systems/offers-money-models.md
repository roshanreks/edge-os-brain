## Why This System Exists

Most Indian D2C brands treat "offer" as a discount code.

Hormozi's point, translated into EDGE OS:

- An **offer** is the full commercial package: product + promise + proof + bonuses + guarantee + price + payment terms.
- A **money model** is the *sequence* of offers that gets a customer to spend more, faster, more often.
- The goal is not a prettier PDP. The goal is: **gross profit from one customer covers the cost to acquire the next customer inside a short payback window.**

Hormozi's operational target: make more profit per customer than it costs to acquire them, ideally inside 30 days.

For Indian D2C that 30-day clock is harsher because of COD remittance lag, RTO, and inventory lock. So the money model must improve cash, not just AOV on a dashboard.

## The Two Books, One Operating Stack

### $100M Offers — make the first yes obvious
Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no.

Core pieces:
1. Find a starving crowd (pain + purchasing power + easy to target + growing).
2. Build a Grand Slam Offer (category of one, premium value, unbeatable guarantee, money model that can fund CAC).
3. Raise value with the Value Equation.
4. Enhance with bonuses, scarcity, urgency, naming, guarantees.
5. Charge based on value, not competitor price lists.

### $100M Money Models — make the yes pay for growth
A money model is a sequence of offers:

**Attraction → Upsell → Downsell → Continuity**

Three cash stages:
1. **Get cash** — attraction offer turns strangers into customers.
2. **Get more cash** — upsells and downsells raise cash collected now.
3. **Get the most cash** — continuity / refill / repeat makes LTV compound.

## The Value Equation

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice)
```

To make an offer stronger, do all four:

| Lever | Raise / lower | Indian D2C examples |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Raise | Clearer transformation: "hair that doesn't frizz in humidity", not "premium shampoo" |
| Likelihood | Raise | Reviews, UGC, demo, founder, lab/ingredient proof, "used by 12,000 customers" |
| Time delay | Lower | Visible first win in 3–14 days, same-day/next-day dispatch, instant unboxing ritual |
| Effort and sacrifice | Lower | Simple routine, one-step use, prepaid incentive, easy returns, COD option, size/fit guide |

Perception is the product. If the customer does not *feel* higher odds, faster result, and less hassle, the offer is still weak.

**EDGE OS rule:** If the founder says "our offer is 20% off", they do not have an offer. They have a leak.

## Grand Slam Offer Checklist

A Grand Slam Offer should:
- Be hard to compare to a commodity alternative
- Solve the next problems, not only the first problem
- Stack bonuses that remove objections
- Reverse risk with a real, believable guarantee
- Support a premium or at least a protected price
- Produce enough cash/margin to fund the next customer

Build sequence (Hormozi, adapted):
1. List every problem around the purchase (trust, fit, results, delivery, returns, "will my family judge this", "will COD waste my time").
2. Turn each problem into a solution / bonus / proof module / guarantee.
3. **Trim and stack:** keep high-value, low-incremental-cost pieces. Kill high-cost, low-perceived-value extras.
4. Name the bundle so it is a *system*, not a SKU.

If customers paid 10x, what would we add? If they paid 1/10th, what is the minimum vehicle? That thought process creates kits, trials, VIP, and continuity.

## The Four Offer Types

### 1. Attraction Offer
Job: turn a stranger into a first buyer without destroying margin forever.

Use to:
- Lower the first "yes"
- Create a reason to try
- Get a customer into the money model

Do **not** use to:
- Permanently cheapen the brand
- Buy fake volume that RTO then eats
- Discount the hero with no next offer

### 2. Upsell
Job: immediately solve the *next* problem the first purchase created.

Three upsell directions:
- **More** of what they bought (2-pack, 3-month supply)
- **Better** version (premium kit, larger size, pro variant)
- **Complement** they now need (moisturizer after serum, wash bag after sneakers)

### 3. Downsell
Job: recover the person who said no, without training the market to wait for 50% off.

Change:
- How much they pay now vs later
- Quality / quantity / features
- Risk (smaller trial, weaker guarantee, fewer bonuses)

### 4. Continuity
Job: keep them buying on a rhythm.

For D2C this is usually:
- Subscribe and save
- Refill reminders
- Consumable replenishment
- Membership / VIP / club
- Seasonal standing order (festive hamper club, monthly snack box)

## The 16 Money-Model Plays (Official Hormozi Map)

Source taxonomy: Acquisition.com *$100M Money Models* training. Translated below into Indian D2C vehicles.

### Attraction plays

| Play | Hormozi idea | India D2C version | Best level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win Your Money Back | Do X, get Y by date Z, get money back / credit | "Use for 14 days. If hair/skin/fit doesn't improve, store credit or refund." Must be trackable and honest. | 2–5 |
| Free Giveaways | Big prize to capture attention + contact; everyone else gets a smaller win | Creator/WhatsApp giveaway. Grand prize + 10–30% of margin participation offer for non-winners. 3–7 day window. | 1–4 |
| Decoy Offer | Cheap/limited version exists so the real offer looks obvious | ₹199 trial vs ₹999 routine kit. Show side-by-side. Ask: "free stuff or lasting result?" | 1–4 |
| Buy X Get Y | Make Y feel bigger than X | Buy 2 get 1, kit + free travel minis, festive BOGO. Raise list price first if needed. Better than a raw 66% off. | 1–5 |
| Pay Less Now | Smaller cash today, more later if they stay / consume | Prepaid save vs COD. BNPL (Simpl/Snapmint) on high AOV. Partial pay for kits. | 2–5 |
| Free with Consumption | Free thing if they commit to using / buying more | Free jar on subscribe. Free gift above ₹999. Free shipping after 2nd prepaid order. | 2–5 |

### Upsell plays

| Play | Hormozi idea | India D2C version | Best level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic Upsell | Can't have X without Y | Serum → moisturizer. Coffee → mug + beans refill. Shoes → extra laces/cleaner. | 2–5 |
| Menu / A/B Upsell | "Which do you want?" not "Do you want this?" | "1 bottle or 2?" "Starter or 90-day?" Card already on file / UPI saved. | 2–5 |
| Unselling | Remove what they don't need to build trust | "You don't need the full 7-product routine. Start with these 2." Then prescribe. | 2–5 |
| Prescription Upsell | Personalized "do this" sells more than a generic add-on | Quiz result: "for oily + humid climate, add this twice a week." | 2–5 |
| Anchor Upsell | Show premium first (often 5–10x), then the core looks reasonable | ₹4,999 complete ritual first, then ₹1,299 hero. Keep the core benefit in both. | 3–5 |
| Rollover Upsell | Credit yesterday's spend into today's bigger offer | "Your last order of ₹799 becomes ₹799 off the ₹2,999 kit today only." Winback + gift-card variants. | 3–5 |
| Stacking Upsells | Sequence 2–4 small yeses after the first purchase | Post-purchase page: warranty/extended exchange → complementary SKU → subscribe. | 3–5 |

### Downsell plays

| Play | Hormozi idea | India D2C version | Best level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payment Plan | Reward paying in full; don't punish installments | Full-pay bonus (free mini). BNPL as recovery, not default. Split pay on ₹3,000+ kits. | 3–5 |
| Trial with Penalty / Free Trial | Start free if they do the work; otherwise they pay | Less common in physical goods. Closest: "try minis, credit into full size if you finish the routine and review." | 3–5 |
| Feature Downsell | Remove a feature/bonus to create a cheaper path | Trial size, no gift, slower shipping, no extended exchange, fewer shades. Name it "Starter", not "Cheap". | 1–5 |

### Continuity plays

| Play | Hormozi idea | India D2C version | Best level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continuity Bonus | Sell the bonus, not the membership | "Subscribe today, get the ritual tin + travel kit." Focus creative on the bonus. | 3–5 |
| Continuity Discount | Discount in exchange for a longer commitment | 10–15% subscribe & save. Apply more of the discount later to reduce early churn-and-run. | 2–5 |
| Waived Fee | Setup / shipping / festival fee waived if they commit | "₹199 delivery waived on 3-month refill." If they cancel early, fee applies. | 3–5 |

## Indian D2C Funnel Map

Do not copy a US info-product webinar funnel onto a ₹799 serum.

Default physical-goods money model:

```
Cold ad (awareness-matched)
  → matching landing page / PDP
    → attraction offer (trial / kit / gift-with-purchase / prepaid perk)
      → checkout (UPI primary, COD as conversion tool not default profit tool)
        → immediate post-purchase upsell (more / better / complement)
          → if no: downsell (smaller size / subscribe / BNPL)
            → WhatsApp + email: education, review, replenishment
              → continuity / refill / second product
                → winback / rollover
```

Awareness match still rules:
- Unaware / Problem Aware → advertorial or quiz, then offer
- Solution Aware → comparison + mechanism + kit
- Product / Most Aware → PDP + offer + guarantee + speed

## Offer Scorecard

Score each live offer 1–5. Below 18/30 is not a Grand Slam.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome clarity | Vague "quality" | Category benefit | Specific, felt transformation |
| Likelihood / proof | No proof | Some reviews | Demo + volume + creator + founder |
| Time to first win | Unclear | Weeks | Days, shown on page |
| Effort / friction | Confusing routine, painful checkout | Average | One-step, mobile-easy, UPI + COD clear |
| Bonus stack | None | 1 weak freebie | 2–4 bonuses that kill objections |
| Risk reversal | Silent returns | Standard 7-day | Named, specific, believable guarantee |
| Next offer ready | Dead end after purchase | Weak related products | Designed upsell + downsell + continuity |
| Cash quality | High COD + high RTO | Mixed | Prepaid mix improving, payback known |

Also track these operating scores:
- **Attraction take rate:** % of sessions that choose the advertised offer
- **Upsell take rate:** % of buyers who accept the next offer (healthy starting target 8–20% on a relevant complement; higher on "1 or 2?")
- **Downsell recovery rate:** % of abandoned / declined buyers who take the smaller path
- **Continuity attach rate:** % of first-time buyers who subscribe or join refill
- **Offer contribution:** revenue and contribution *after* discounts, gifts, RTO, and shipping
- **Payback:** days until contribution covers blended CAC

A high take rate on a stupid discount is not a win.

## What To Install By Level

### Level 1 (0–5L)
One hero. One attraction offer. No circus.

- Buy X Get Y or a clean starter kit
- Simple guarantee
- Guest checkout, UPI + COD
- Do not build 4 upsells on an unproven product

### Level 2 (5–20L)
Turn the first purchase into a second purchase.

- Offer matrix: starter / bundle / prepaid perk / guarantee
- Classic + menu upsell on cart and post-purchase
- Feature downsell: trial size
- Basic WhatsApp replenishment

### Level 3 (20–50L)
Money model becomes a system.

- Attraction offer for cold traffic, different from retargeting offer
- Post-purchase stack (2 steps max at first)
- Subscribe & save on consumables
- Decoy (trial vs ritual) on advertorials
- Score take rates weekly

### Level 4 (50L–1Cr)
Industrialize.

- Anchor kits and VIP bundles
- Rollover winbacks
- Continuity bonuses and waived-fee shipping
- Channel-specific offers (marketplace vs D2C vs q-comm)
- Offer-level P&L

### Level 5 (1Cr+)
Portfolio money models.

- Different models per product line
- Association / celebrity used to sell the *system*, not a random SKU
- Continuity as a real P&L line
- Experiment budget for new attraction vehicles
- Never let promotional calendar destroy the core offer

## Rules That Prevent Hormozi-From-Breaking-India

1. **Do not fake guarantees.** If RTO is already 25%, "money back no questions" without process will kill cash.
2. **Do not use COD as the attraction offer.** COD is a trust tool. Prepaid perk is the offer.
3. **Gifts must have a job.** Free pouch that doesn't remove an objection is just COGS.
4. **Never discount the core first.** Add bonuses. If you must cut price, cut features.
5. **One money model per hero product** before a new SKU.
6. **Measure contribution, not only conversion.** An upsell that raises RTO is a downsell in disguise.
7. **Festive scarcity must be real.** Fake timers train distrust in Tier 2/3 buyers.
8. **Unselling builds Indian trust.** Prescribing less can sell more than dumping 8 add-ons.

## Offer Working Session (90 minutes)

1. Name the dream outcome in one sentence.
2. List the 10 reasons a buyer does not purchase.
3. Attach a bonus, proof, or guarantee to each reason.
4. Trim anything expensive that customers do not value.
5. Design the next offer (upsell) and the smaller yes (downsell).
6. Decide the continuity hook if the product is consumed.
7. Score the offer. If under 18, do not scale spend.
