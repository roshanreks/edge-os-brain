# Association & Celebrity System

Association (influencers, celebrities, brand partnerships, cultural affiliations) is one of the five EDGE components.

## What Happens When You Use the Right Association
- Immediate awareness and brand lift
- Lower effective long-term CAC
- Higher trust and perceived quality
- Ability to profitably reach broader (more Unaware) audiences
- Content that can be cut into many high-performing ads

## What Does Not Happen When You Avoid It
- You stay trapped longer in pure performance marketing
- Creative fatigue hits harder
- Expanding total addressable market becomes harder
- Brand equity compounds more slowly

## Practical Rules by Level
- **Level 1–2:** Heavy nano and micro influencers + founder + customer UGC. Best ROI and authenticity.
- **Level 3:** Scale micro programs and start selective mid-tier.
- **Level 4–5:** Strategic mid-to-macro and carefully chosen celebrity moments for brand and category expansion. Still keep the micro engine running.

Celebrity is expensive and attribution is messy. It works best when it multiplies an already working EDGE rather than trying to create one from nothing.

Nano/micro often outperform pure celebrity on engagement, trust, and usable UGC volume.
