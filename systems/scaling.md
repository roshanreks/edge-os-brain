## The Scaling Law

Scale what is working. Fix what is blocking. Ignore what is not the constraint.

Hormozi: get more customers, get them to pay more, get them to buy more times.
EDGE OS: extract the edge, then multiply it.

Scaling is not "increase budget 20%." Scaling is raising **throughput of a proven system** until a new constraint appears.

## Do Not Scale If

From the metrics brain, still true:
- You cannot name contribution margin or break-even CAC
- Relevant traffic CVR is under ~1% and the page is confused
- One ad and one SKU carry everything and you have no pipeline
- Fulfillment or inventory will snap
- COD RTO is eating the profit
- Cash payback is longer than runway
- Support tickets spike when spend spikes

If any of these are red, scale is vanity.

## Three Directions of Scale

1. **More of the same winner** — more spend, more inventory, more hours on the winning angle (vertical).
2. **More versions of the winner** — hooks, formats, creators, landing pages on the same edge (horizontal).
3. **More ways they pay** — upsell, kit, continuity, prepaid mix (money model).

Most brands only do #1 and then say "Meta died."

## The Constraint Ladder

When you try to scale, one of these breaks first. Fix that one.

1. Offer / product truth
2. Creative volume and diversity
3. Landing page / CVR / message match
4. Checkout / UPI / COD / speed
5. RTO / fulfillment / CX
6. Repeat / cash conversion
7. Inventory / working capital
8. Team / founder bottleneck
9. Channel concentration

Advice that ignores the current constraint is noise.

## How To Scale Paid

### Vertical (more budget on winners)
- Raise spend in steps the account and cash can absorb (often 10–20% every few days, not 5x overnight)
- Watch new-customer CPA, MER, contribution, frequency, and RTO together
- If efficiency dies immediately, you have a creative or offer problem, not a "learning phase" religion

### Horizontal (more winners)
- This is the real scale path after L2
- CTC: you need enough tests to find the next 3.5% outlier
- Expand awareness stages so you are not farming the same warm pool
- New creator ≠ new concept unless the angle changes

### Structural
- Separate testing and scaling
- Protect testing budget while scaling
- Andromeda / modern Meta: creative diversity *is* targeting
- Google / YouTube / marketplaces / q-comm are L3+ diversification, not L1 homework

## How To Scale Offers (Hormozi)

When CAC rises, do not only buy more traffic.

- Raise AOV with menu and classic upsells
- Raise cash quality with prepaid and continuity
- Raise conversion with a stronger Grand Slam stack (bonuses + guarantee + naming)
- Raise LTV with second product and refill
- Use rollover to reactivate old buyers cheaper than new ones

A brand that scales spend without a money model is renting revenue.

## How To Scale Operations

Every rupee of new media needs:
- Stock
- Pack
- Ship
- COD verify
- NDR recover
- Answer WhatsApp
- Process returns

If any of those are founder-in-the-WhatsApp, you cannot scale past L3 cleanly.

## How To Scale Team

| Level | Founder still owns | First hires / freelancers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Everything | Occasional editor / UGC |
| 2 | Taste + offers + money | Editor, CS, ops help |
| 3 | Edge and weekly review | Creative cadence, media, CX lead |
| 4 | System architecture | Pod: creative, media, ops, finance |
| 5 | Category and capital | Leaders with scorecards |

Do not hire an agency to find the edge. Hire help to multiply a named edge.

## Level Scaling Jobs

| Level | Scale this | Do not scale this |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learning speed, conversations, cheap concepts | Spend, team, SKUs |
| 2 | Winning angle volume, offer matrix | Celebrity, new categories |
| 3 | Creative engine + cold-traffic pages + RTO system | Random channels |
| 4 | Brand codes, retention, multi-channel, association tests | Founder-as-bottleneck heroics |
| 5 | Portfolio, category narrative, capital-efficient experiments | Growth at any MER |

## Scaling Scoreboard (weekly)

- New vs returning revenue
- Blended CAC and MER
- Contribution after ads, RTO, discounts
- Creative: launched / activated / scaled / killed
- Offer attach rates
- Inventory weeks of cover
- Cash conversion (COD outstanding, refunds)
- CX backlog

If spend is up and this board is not reviewed, you are not scaling. You are hoping.

## Festival / Sale Scaling

India has forced scale windows (Amazon/Flipkart sales, Diwali, Holi, Rakhi, wedding season).

Rules:
- Creative and inventory decisions happen weeks earlier
- Attraction offers can get more aggressive; core price should survive November
- Ops and COD verification must be staffed first
- Do not judge a brand only on festive ROAS
- After the spike, continuity and rollover harvest the new buyers
