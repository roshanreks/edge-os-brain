# Conversion Rate (CVR) & Store System

Use this file as the quick CVR overview. For full store, PDP, landing page, funnel, and CRO workflow, see `systems/conversion-cro-store-funnels.md`.

## India D2C Shopify Benchmarks (2026)

- Overall average: ~1.5–1.8%
- Top quartile: 2.5–3.5%+ (category dependent)
- Fashion: typically 1.2–2.5%
- Beauty / Personal Care: 1.5–2.8%
- Food / consumables: higher
- Mobile CVR is the major leak (often 1.0–1.5% vs desktop 2.5–3.5%)

A 0.5–1% absolute improvement in CVR is frequently higher leverage than large increases in ad spend.

## Highest-Leverage CVR Levers (in rough order)
1. Mobile checkout friction (UPI primary, COD verification, address experience)
2. Above-the-fold trust + social proof
3. Offer clarity (price, free shipping threshold, bundles)
4. Page speed (< 2.5–3 seconds)
5. PDP structure that matches the ad angle (problem → solution → proof → offer)
6. Size guides / quizzes / recommendation tools (category dependent)
7. Exit-intent and cart recovery (WhatsApp + email)

## Awareness Matching
Landing pages and PDPs must match the awareness level of the traffic.  
Cold traffic needs more education and proof.  
Hot traffic needs less friction and stronger offer.

## Store Design Principles
- Speed and clarity beat pure aesthetics
- Mobile-first always
- Trust elements visible without scrolling
- Consistent with the winning creative messaging
