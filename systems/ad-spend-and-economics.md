# Ad Spend & Unit Economics System

## Ad Spend as % of Revenue (India D2C 2026 calibrated)

| Level | Monthly Revenue | Typical Ad Spend % | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|--------------------|-------|
| 1 | 0–5 Lacs | 30–50% | Validation mode |
| 2 | 5–20 Lacs | 25–35% | Still growth-heavy |
| 3 | 20–50 Lacs | 20–28% | Retention must start helping |
| 4 | 50L–1 Cr | 15–22% | Brand + organic + retention carry more |
| 5 | 1 Cr+ | 12–18% | Efficiency + moat |

These percentages only work if contribution margin and repeat rate are healthy. Pure ROAS without looking at LTV and cash is dangerous.

## Key Unit Economics Truths (Indian D2C)
- Most brands are unprofitable on the first order
- Profitability usually arrives at order 2–3
- Brands with 25%+ 90-day repeat rates have dramatically better margins
- CAC has been rising (Meta especially)
- COD still material; RTO is a major silent killer of margins

## Capital Allocation Priority (General)
1. Proven winners (inventory + media to scale them)
2. Creative testing engine
3. Retention / second-purchase systems
4. Fulfillment reliability & packaging
5. Brand / association plays
6. New product experiments

## Cash Flow Reality
India e-com is working-capital intensive because of COD + returns. Improving cash conversion cycle (faster verification, lower RTO, better inventory turns) is often higher leverage than pure top-line growth.
