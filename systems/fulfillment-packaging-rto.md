# Fulfillment, Packaging, COD, and RTO

Fulfillment is not back-office work in Indian D2C. It is part of product experience, cash flow, contribution margin, and brand trust.

## Core Principle

A sale is not complete when the order is placed. It is complete when the customer receives the product, keeps it, uses it, and is willing to buy again.

## Key Terms

- **COD:** Cash on delivery. Useful for conversion in India, but can increase RTO and cash delay.
- **RTO:** Return to origin. Order ships but returns undelivered.
- **NDR:** Non-delivery report. Courier could not deliver and needs action.
- **Prepaid share:** Percentage of orders paid before delivery.
- **Delivery SLA:** Expected delivery time.

## What To Track

Track by SKU, source, campaign, offer, city tier, courier, payment mode, and customer cohort:
- orders placed
- prepaid vs COD
- shipped
- delivered
- cancelled before ship
- RTO
- NDR
- damaged/lost
- refund/exchange
- support tickets
- delivery time
- repeat purchase after delivery

## RTO Reduction Levers

- COD confirmation via WhatsApp/call/OTP
- address validation
- pincode serviceability checks
- prepaid incentives
- clear product expectation on PDP
- accurate size/material/usage information
- faster dispatch
- NDR follow-up
- courier performance review
- customer support before delivery
- fraud/risk scoring
- excluding high-risk segments when economics demand it

## RTO Diagnosis

Ask:
- Is RTO higher on COD than prepaid?
- Is it concentrated in certain cities, pincodes, couriers, or SKUs?
- Is the ad overpromising?
- Is the offer attracting low-intent buyers?
- Is the delivery timeline unclear?
- Are customers confused after ordering?
- Is support failing during NDR?
- Are there product expectation gaps?

## Packaging

Packaging has four jobs:
1. protect the product
2. set expectation and perceived value
3. create a memorable receiving experience
4. support repeat purchase, referral, or content if economics allow

## Packaging by Level

| Level | Packaging focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | safe, clean, basic brand consistency |
| 2 | better inserts, review/referral prompts, unboxing basics |
| 3 | damage reduction, product education, retention inserts |
| 4 | stronger brand experience, creator/unboxing assets if viable |
| 5 | packaging as brand code, sustainability/quality systems, cost optimization |

## Inserts and Post-Purchase Materials

Useful inserts:
- how to use
- what to expect
- QR to routine/demo video
- review request
- replenishment reminder
- referral
- founder note
- care instructions
- cross-sell suggestion
- support contact

Avoid inserts that create clutter without improving usage, retention, or trust.

## Fulfillment and Creative Connection

Ops data should feed creative:
- high return reason -> ad/PDP objection section
- common usage confusion -> demo creative
- delivery trust issue -> store proof and WhatsApp flow
- low repeat because product misunderstood -> post-purchase education
- high RTO from offer traffic -> revise offer/ad promise

## Level-Specific Fulfillment Focus

| Level | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | reliable shipping, basic COD confirmation, honest PDP |
| 2 | RTO tracking, support scripts, delivery clarity |
| 3 | dashboards by source/SKU/courier, NDR process, prepaid incentives |
| 4 | SOPs, courier optimization, packaging strategy, CX feedback loop |
| 5 | forecasting, segmentation, automation, cost negotiation, brand experience |

## Fulfillment Review Checklist

- What is RTO by source, SKU, courier, and payment mode?
- Which ads create the worst order quality?
- Which products create the most support tickets?
- Where is cash delayed?
- What is the cost of RTO including forward and reverse logistics?
- Are customer expectations clear before purchase?
- Are NDRs acted on fast enough?
- Does packaging reduce damage and improve perceived value?
- Are support insights entering product, CRO, and creative decisions?
