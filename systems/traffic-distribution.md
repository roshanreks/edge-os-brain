# Traffic and Distribution

Distribution is the fifth EDGE component: how more people come to know about the brand.

Paid ads are not the whole distribution system. They are one way to buy attention and test demand. As the brand grows, distribution must include owned, earned, creator, community, search, marketplace, partnership, and offline surfaces where relevant.

## Distribution Principle

The brand should not depend forever on one platform, one campaign, one audience, or one ad type.

But distribution expansion should happen in sequence. A Level 1 brand does not need 12 channels. A Level 5 brand should not be dependent on only one.

## Core Channels

### Paid Social
Examples: Meta, TikTok where available, YouTube Shorts, influencer whitelisting.

Best for:
- fast creative testing
- demand capture and creation
- founder/content-led products
- visual products

Watch:
- creative fatigue
- CAC
- MER
- traffic quality
- RTO by source

### Paid Search / Shopping
Best for:
- demand capture
- competitor/category searches
- high-intent buyers
- branded search defense

Watch:
- incrementality
- brand vs non-brand mix
- landing page relevance

### Organic Social
Best for:
- founder POV
- community building
- cultural relevance
- creator proof
- testing hooks without media spend

Watch:
- consistency
- audience quality
- conversion path

### Creators and Influencers
Best for:
- content volume
- trust transfer
- niche community access
- proof
- new personas

Watch:
- content rights
- briefing quality
- performance vs vanity metrics

### Email, WhatsApp, SMS
Best for:
- retention
- abandoned cart
- post-purchase education
- replenishment
- launches
- winback

Watch:
- list quality
- deliverability
- unsubscribes
- over-discounting

### SEO and Content
Best for:
- education-heavy categories
- comparison searches
- problem-aware traffic
- long-term demand capture

Watch:
- time horizon
- content quality
- conversion path

### Marketplaces
Best for:
- discovery
- trust-sensitive buyers
- category search
- volume

Watch:
- margin
- customer ownership
- review quality
- channel conflict

### Offline / Retail / Events
Best for:
- sampling
- trust
- premium products
- local density
- category education

Watch:
- working capital
- sell-through
- retailer terms
- field execution

### Partnerships and PR
Best for:
- credibility
- new audiences
- association
- brand memory

Watch:
- vague exposure without usable assets
- lack of follow-up funnel

## Level-Specific Distribution

| Level | Distribution focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Meta/paid social + founder/organic + basic WhatsApp/email |
| 2 | Scale paid social, creators for content, branded search, retention flows |
| 3 | Add landing pages, search/shopping, creator clusters, organic cadence, SEO tests |
| 4 | Multi-channel testing, PR/association, marketplace/offline if economics fit |
| 5 | Portfolio distribution: paid, owned, earned, creators, search, marketplace, offline, partnerships |

## Distribution Expansion Rule

Add a channel only when:
- the core offer is proven
- the channel has a clear role
- the landing path is ready
- the tracking or measurement logic is defined
- there is an owner
- there is a kill rule and scale rule

## Traffic Quality

Not all traffic is equal. Judge traffic by:
- CVR
- AOV
- CAC
- contribution margin
- RTO/cancellation rate
- repeat purchase rate
- support burden
- customer quality

A channel that looks cheap but creates bad orders is expensive.

## Traffic to Funnel Fit

| Traffic type | Funnel need |
|---|---|
| Cold entertainment | story, education, content bridge |
| Cold problem-aware | advertorial, quiz, problem landing page |
| Search high-intent | PDP, comparison, category page |
| Creator traffic | creator-matched landing page or PDP with proof |
| Retargeting | PDP, offer, testimonial, objection handling |
| Existing customers | replenishment, cross-sell, bundle, loyalty |

## Distribution Review Checklist

- What channel creates the best new customer contribution?
- What channel creates the best repeat customers?
- Which channel has the highest RTO?
- Which channel depends most on one creative?
- Which channel builds owned audience?
- Which channel improves brand memory?
- Which channel is only vanity?
- Which channel should be killed, capped, or scaled?
