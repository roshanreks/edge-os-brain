# Diagnostic Engine

This is the standard process for diagnosing an e-commerce brand owner and turning their current situation into a precise EDGE OS roadmap.

## Required Inputs

Ask for:
- monthly revenue range
- category
- AOV
- hero product or SKU mix
- gross margin
- contribution margin if known
- monthly ad spend
- main channels
- CAC/CPA or blended ROAS/MER
- store conversion rate
- repeat purchase rate
- prepaid vs COD split
- RTO/cancellation rate
- creative testing volume per month
- creator/founder content volume
- email/WhatsApp revenue or flow status
- team size
- inventory/cash constraint
- biggest felt problem

If numbers are missing, diagnose with confidence levels and state what must be measured next.

## Step 1: Identify Level

Use monthly revenue:
- Level 1: 0-5 Lacs
- Level 2: 5-20 Lacs
- Level 3: 20-50 Lacs
- Level 4: 50 Lacs-1 Cr
- Level 5: 1 Cr+

Then check if the brand behaves like that level. A 30 Lac brand with no creative system may need Level 2 discipline before Level 3 expansion.

## Step 2: Score EDGE

Score each from 1-5:

| Component | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Product | repeat, reviews, refunds, differentiation, usage |
| Branding | positioning, memory, trust, visual consistency, POV |
| Creative | volume, angles, performance, awareness coverage |
| Association | creators, experts, celebrities, PR, partnerships |
| Distribution | channel mix, traffic quality, owned audience, reach |

The lowest score often reveals the current bottleneck.

For deeper scoring, use `systems/growth-scorecards.md`.

## Step 3: Awareness Coverage

Map current ads/content/store to:
- Unaware
- Problem Aware
- Solution Aware
- Product Aware
- Most Aware

Most stuck brands over-rely on Product Aware and Most Aware messages. Growth past Level 3 usually requires colder-stage education and memory building.

## Step 4: Unit Economics

Inspect:
- gross margin
- AOV
- CAC/CPA
- ad spend as % revenue
- contribution margin
- shipping/COD/RTO cost
- discount rate
- repeat purchase
- payback timing

If economics are unclear, the first roadmap item is measurement.

## Step 5: Creative System

Inspect:
- concepts per month
- angle families
- low-effort vs high-effort mix
- founder content
- creator content
- testing budget
- naming/tagging
- weekly review
- ad-to-page match
- fatigue and refresh cadence

## Step 6: Store and Funnel

Inspect:
- mobile CVR
- PDP clarity
- proof depth
- page speed
- checkout friction
- offer clarity
- landing page fit by traffic temperature
- product education
- reviews/UGC
- COD/shipping/return clarity

## Step 7: Fulfillment and Cash

Inspect:
- RTO
- COD share
- NDR process
- delivery time
- support tickets
- packaging damage
- inventory turns
- cash conversion cycle
- supplier lead time
- dead stock

## Output Format

Use this format for founder diagnosis:

1. **Level:** current level and why
2. **Main bottleneck:** one sentence
3. **EDGE Score:** Product, Branding, Creative, Association, Distribution
4. **Department Scores:** Product, Creative, Copywriting, Offer/Funnel, CRO/Store, Product Development, Fulfillment/Cash
5. **Awareness map:** strongest and weakest stages
6. **Unit economics read:** what looks healthy/unhealthy/missing
7. **90-day priorities:** 3-5 ranked moves
8. **What to stop:** actions that waste attention or cash
9. **Metrics to watch:** exact weekly scoreboard
10. **Next assets to build:** ads, pages, flows, SOPs, dashboards

## Prioritization Logic

Use this hierarchy:

1. Fix measurement if the founder cannot see reality.
2. Fix product/offer if there is no demand or repeat signal.
3. Fix creative volume and message if CAC is rising.
4. Fix store if traffic is decent but CVR is weak.
5. Fix fulfillment/RTO if order quality is poor.
6. Fix retention if first-order acquisition is working but LTV is weak.
7. Expand distribution only after the core engine is stable.
8. Use association/celebrity only when it multiplies a proven edge.
9. Use offer architecture once the product promise is clear: attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity.
10. Use product development only after the product role, economics, and launch stage gates are clear.

## Common Diagnostic Patterns

### "Sales are stuck despite spending"
Likely causes:
- creative fatigue
- narrow awareness stage
- weak PDP match
- CAC too high for margin
- no retention

### "ROAS looks good but cash is tight"
Likely causes:
- COD/RTO delay
- inventory payments
- discounts
- weak contribution margin
- platform reporting hiding blended reality

### "Lots of traffic, few orders"
Likely causes:
- wrong traffic
- slow mobile site
- unclear offer
- weak proof
- price/trust objection
- ad promise mismatch

### "Customers buy once and disappear"
Likely causes:
- product expectation gap
- weak post-purchase education
- no replenishment/cross-sell
- product category has low natural repeat
- acquisition attracting wrong buyers

### "We want a celebrity"
First check:
- Is the product proven?
- Is the store ready?
- Is creative volume already healthy?
- Is there a content usage plan?
- Is there a landing page/proof plan?
- What business role will the association play?
