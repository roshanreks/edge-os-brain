# Copywriting Engine

Copywriting in EDGE OS is not "clever words." It is the bridge between customer awareness, product truth, and the action we want the buyer to take.

The best copy is built from diagnosis:
- Who is the customer?
- What do they already believe?
- What pain or desire is active?
- Which awareness stage are they in?
- What proof do they need next?
- What objection stops purchase?
- What offer makes action feel easy?

## The Core Copy Map

Every piece of copy should be mapped to:

| Layer | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Who is this for? | Persona, identity, situation |
| Pain/desire | What do they want changed? | Problem, aspiration, trigger |
| Awareness | How ready are they? | Unaware to Most Aware |
| Mechanism | Why does this work differently? | Product edge, process, ingredient, design |
| Proof | Why should they believe it? | Reviews, demo, expert, creator, data, founder |
| Offer | Why act now? | Bundle, guarantee, price, shipping, urgency |
| Risk reversal | What fear is reduced? | Return, warranty, COD, trial, support |

## Eugene Schwartz Awareness in Practice

Use `core/schwartz-awareness-levels.md` as the base model.

### 1. Unaware

The customer is not searching for the product or problem. Copy must earn attention through identity, curiosity, entertainment, a cultural truth, or a surprising observation.

Good for:
- broad social content
- founder POV
- skits/funny ads
- lifestyle hooks
- category creation
- Level 4-5 demand creation

Copy jobs:
- make them recognize themselves
- introduce a hidden tension
- create curiosity before selling
- attach the brand to a lifestyle or belief

### 2. Problem Aware

The customer feels the pain but has not chosen a solution type.

Copy jobs:
- name the pain more precisely than they can
- show the cost of ignoring it
- explain why common fixes fail
- create urgency without fake panic

Example angles:
- "Why your skin still feels dry even after moisturizer"
- "The reason your COD orders keep getting returned"
- "The morning routine mistake that ruins your hair by lunch"

### 3. Solution Aware

The customer knows the category of solution but not your product.

Copy jobs:
- explain the unique mechanism
- compare alternatives
- prove why your version is better, easier, safer, faster, or more desirable
- give the buyer a buying criterion

Example angles:
- "Cotton vs bamboo for daily wear"
- "Why this format absorbs better than capsules"
- "The 3 things to check before buying a protein snack"

### 4. Product Aware

The customer knows the product but has not bought.

Copy jobs:
- overcome objections
- deepen proof
- show use cases
- clarify price/value
- remove risk

Example angles:
- "Will this work for oily skin?"
- "What customers noticed after 14 days"
- "Watch the product in real use"

### 5. Most Aware

The customer knows the product and is close to buying.

Copy jobs:
- present the offer clearly
- create honest urgency
- make checkout easy
- reduce final hesitation

Example angles:
- "Buy 2, get free shipping"
- "Back in stock"
- "Try it risk-free"

## Market Sophistication

As a category matures, claims become less believable. EDGE OS uses a simplified version of market sophistication:

| Stage | Market condition | Copy move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | People have not heard the claim | State the core benefit simply |
| 2 | Competitors repeat similar claims | Intensify or specify the claim |
| 3 | Claims feel familiar | Introduce a unique mechanism |
| 4 | Mechanisms are copied | Make the mechanism more specific, credible, and ownable |
| 5 | Market is exhausted | Lead with identity, belief, association, proof, and brand world |

At Level 1-2, simple direct response can work. At Level 3-5, copy must carry more mechanism, proof, story, and brand memory because the audience has seen similar offers before.

## Copy Assets Every Brand Needs

### Customer Profile
- Demographic: age, gender, city tier, income band, life stage
- Situation: where the problem appears in daily life
- Desire: what they want to feel, become, avoid, or signal
- Objections: price, trust, efficacy, fit, delivery, returns, social judgement
- Language: exact phrases from reviews, comments, support tickets, WhatsApp, Reddit, Amazon, marketplace reviews

### Messaging House
- One-sentence promise
- Category enemy
- Unique mechanism
- Proof stack
- Offer stack
- Objection answers
- Founder POV
- Brand tone
- Words to use and avoid

### Claims Library
Every claim should have a proof type:
- demonstrable proof
- customer proof
- expert proof
- ingredient/material proof
- process proof
- founder story proof
- comparison proof
- guarantee/risk reversal

Do not scale claims the brand cannot defend.

## Copy Formats

### Ad Hook
The first 1-3 seconds or first line. It should stop the right person.

Hook types:
- pain callout
- identity callout
- curiosity gap
- contradiction
- demonstration
- comparison
- confession
- social proof
- objection
- offer
- trend/cultural reference

### Body Copy
The body must move the customer one step closer:
- educate
- prove
- show
- compare
- answer
- dramatize
- invite

### CTA
The CTA should match awareness:
- Unaware: "See why", "Watch this", "Learn more"
- Problem Aware: "Find the fix", "See the routine"
- Solution Aware: "Compare options", "See how it works"
- Product Aware: "Read reviews", "Choose your kit"
- Most Aware: "Buy now", "Get the offer", "Restock today"

## Copy Review Checklist

- Is the target customer obvious?
- Is the awareness stage clear?
- Does the copy say something specific, not generic?
- Is the product edge visible?
- Is there enough proof for the claim?
- Does the offer feel clear?
- Are the main objections answered?
- Does the CTA match the buyer's readiness?
- Does the copy sound like the customer language, not boardroom language?
- Can this be turned into at least 5 creative variants?

## Landing Page Copy Planning

Before writing a landing page, define:
- customer
- problem
- angle
- awareness stage
- product role
- offer
- proof sequence
- objections
- next step after purchase

The page should not start with what the founder wants to say. It should start with what the buyer needs to understand next.

## Offer Copy

Offer copy should make value easy to understand without creating confusion.

Write:
- what they get
- why it matters
- why it is better now/in this format
- who it is for
- what risk is removed
- what happens after purchase

Avoid:
- fake urgency
- generic benefit claims
- bundles that are hard to understand
- clever offer names that hide the actual value
- guarantees the operations team cannot support

## Practical Workflow

1. Pull raw language from reviews, comments, support, sales calls, and founder notes.
2. Sort into pain, desire, objection, proof, outcome, comparison, identity.
3. Map each insight to an awareness stage.
4. Write 10 hooks per insight.
5. Turn the best hooks into statics, scripts, PDP sections, email, WhatsApp, and landing page modules.
6. Use performance data and customer feedback to update the messaging house weekly.

## Hormozi Offer Copy (use with `systems/offers-money-models.md`)

Copy does not only describe the product. Copy presents the **Grand Slam stack**.

### Value-equation lines
- Dream outcome: what life looks like after
- Likelihood: why this works for someone like them
- Time: first win in days, not "eventually"
- Effort: how little they must do

Fast beats free. "See a difference in 7 humid days" beats "complimentary consultation energy".

### Bonus copy
Each bonus gets:
- A name
- The problem it removes
- A proof scrap
- A picture of life after using it

Never present a nameless free pouch.

### Guarantee copy
Formula: **If you don't get X in Y, we will Z.**

Weak: "100% satisfaction"
Stronger: "Use 14 nights. If your skin is still reacting, we refund or credit the ritual kit. Photo of the empty mini is enough."

Conditional guarantees belong in India more often than blank cheques.

### Menu / prescription lines
- "You don't need the 7-step. You need these two."
- "One bottle or two — most first-time oily-skin buyers start with two because one finishes in 18 days."
- "If you already own a cleanser, skip the kit and take the serum."

Unselling is copy. It builds trust and still sells.

### Naming
Before you rebuild the product, rename the wrapper:
- Avatar + goal + interval + container
- Examples: "14-Day Monsoon Barrier Reset", "Office 5pm Protein Protocol", "Rakhi Gifting System"

Then refresh creative, then body, then the enhancer (bonus / date / cap).

## Copy Scores

| Question | Fail | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Could a stranger say who this is for? | Generic "you" | Named situation |
| Is the mechanism visible? | "Premium" | Specific how |
| Is there proof next to the claim? | Claim orphan | Claim + evidence |
| Does the offer have a name? | "20% off" | Named kit / guarantee |
| Does CTA match awareness? | Buy now on a problem ad | See the routine / compare / buy |
| Can this spawn 5 creatives? | One-off slogan | Angle family |
