# Ad Types Library

This file is the working library of ad formats EDGE OS can recommend by level, awareness stage, and business constraint.

The goal is not to make "nice ads." The goal is to create more shots at discovering and multiplying the brand's edge.

## Core Ad Type Matrix

| Ad type | Best awareness stage | Best level | Primary job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product demo | Product Aware, Solution Aware | 1-5 | Show what it is and why it works |
| Problem/solution | Problem Aware | 1-5 | Name pain and introduce fix |
| Founder talking head | Unaware, Problem, Product | 1-5 | Build trust and POV |
| UGC testimonial | Product Aware | 1-5 | Transfer customer proof |
| Before/after | Problem, Product | 1-5 | Make outcome visible |
| Comparison | Solution Aware | 2-5 | Create buying criteria |
| Objection handling | Product Aware | 2-5 | Remove hesitation |
| Offer ad | Most Aware | 1-5 | Harvest demand |
| Listicle/static carousel | Problem, Solution | 1-5 | Educate quickly |
| Skit/funny ad | Unaware, Problem | 2-5 | Earn attention through relatability |
| Expert proof | Solution, Product | 3-5 | Increase credibility |
| Creator routine | Unaware, Problem, Product | 2-5 | Show product in life context |
| Celebrity/influencer association | Unaware, Product | 4-5 | Transfer trust, reach, and memory |
| Advertorial | Problem, Solution | 3-5 | Educate colder traffic |
| Quiz/guided selling | Problem, Solution | 3-5 | Help customer self-identify |
| Brand film | Unaware | 4-5 | Build memory and identity |

## Low-Effort Content

Low-effort does not mean low-quality thinking. It means fast production, low cost, and high learning speed.

Examples:
- phone-shot UGC
- founder selfie video
- product demo on a table
- customer screenshot static
- review carousel
- screen recording
- simple before/after
- meme-style statics
- text-on-screen hooks
- creator raw clip

Use low-effort content for:
- testing hooks
- testing claims
- testing persona language
- testing objections
- testing product demonstrations
- daily or weekly creative volume

Best levels:
- Level 1: dominant mode
- Level 2: still dominant, with more structure
- Level 3-5: always present as the testing base

## High-Effort Content

High-effort content should usually be made from proven learning, not guesswork.

Examples:
- polished founder story
- scripted multi-scene UGC
- studio demos
- cinematic product visuals
- influencer shoot
- celebrity content
- branded campaign film
- motion graphics explainer
- comparison video with strong art direction

Use high-effort content for:
- scaling proven angles
- brand memory
- association
- launches
- high-AOV categories
- trust-sensitive products
- broad audiences

Best levels:
- Level 1: only when category demands it or assets are cheap
- Level 2: start upgrading winners
- Level 3: convert winning low-effort into polished variants
- Level 4-5: dedicated production lane

## Funny Ads

Funny ads work when the humor is attached to a real customer tension. Random jokes rarely scale.

Good funny ad structures:
- exaggerate the pain
- dramatize the before state
- show the embarrassing truth customers already know
- turn an objection into a scene
- contrast "old way vs new way"
- use a recurring character or situation
- make the product the resolution, not a forced logo at the end

Best for:
- Unaware and Problem Aware audiences
- categories with daily-life pain
- products that need attention before education
- brands with a distinct voice

Avoid:
- humor that makes the customer feel stupid
- humor that distracts from the product
- jokes that cannot be connected to the offer
- trends that dilute brand memory

## Founder Content

Founder content is not only for small brands. It is an authenticity and POV asset at every level.

Founder angles:
- why we made this
- what everyone gets wrong in the category
- product demo from the founder
- customer story
- behind the scenes
- quality control
- mistake/lesson
- myth-busting
- offer explanation
- brand belief

Level use:
- Level 1: founder is the trust engine
- Level 2: founder creates winning angles
- Level 3: founder becomes a recurring proof source
- Level 4: founder POV becomes brand memory
- Level 5: founder is one of many trust assets, but should not disappear

## Creator Content

Creator content is useful for:
- angle discovery
- persona expansion
- social proof
- demonstration in real life
- content volume
- entering new communities

Creator briefs should include:
- customer persona
- awareness stage
- product edge
- one problem
- one proof point
- one objection
- required demo shot
- forbidden claims
- CTA/offer

Do not give creators only a product and a vague instruction to "make content."

## Celebrity / Association Ads

Celebrity content does not automatically create profitable acquisition. It works best when it has a clear role:
- trust transfer
- reach expansion
- memory creation
- social proof
- PR
- category authority
- offline/retail credibility
- performance assets built from the shoot

The asset plan matters more than the face alone.

Before using celebrity, define:
- target audience
- message
- content deliverables
- usage rights
- paid amplification plan
- landing page/store proof placement
- success metric
- fallback if direct conversion is weak

## What To Test

Test one major variable at a time when possible:
- customer persona
- awareness stage
- hook
- product claim
- pain
- mechanism
- proof type
- demonstration
- offer
- price framing
- CTA
- format
- creator archetype
- editing pace
- thumbnail/first frame
- landing page match

## Level-Specific Ad Mix

| Level | Recommended mix |
|---|---|
| 1 | Demo, founder, UGC, problem/solution, reviews, simple statics |
| 2 | More UGC, founder, comparison, objection, offer, early skits |
| 3 | Full angle families, advertorials, creator routines, comparison, problem education |
| 4 | Creative pod output, high-effort winners, association tests, expert proof, landing page funnels |
| 5 | Full-funnel creative system, brand memory, celebrity/creator clusters, new channel creative, retention creative |

## Creative Pass/Fail Signals

Early signals:
- thumbstop or hook rate
- CTR
- CPC
- add-to-cart
- comments and saves
- landing page scroll/click depth

Purchase signals:
- CAC/CPA
- CVR
- AOV
- contribution margin
- RTO/cancellation quality
- repeat cohort quality

Do not scale ads that create low-quality orders, high RTO, or heavy support burden unless the unit economics still work.
