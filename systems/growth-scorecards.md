# Growth Scorecards

Scorecards make EDGE OS diagnostic work consistent. They help a founder understand where the brand is strong, where it is leaking, and what to fix first.

Scores are not absolute truth. They are a structured way to make better decisions.

Also use:
- Offer scorecard in `systems/offers-money-models.md`
- Creative / CRO / product scores in `systems/testing-and-experimentation.md`

## Master EDGE Score (100 Points)

| Component | Points | What to inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Product | 20 | demand, differentiation, repeat, reviews, margin, fulfillment fit |
| Branding | 15 | positioning, customer profile, memory, visual trust, founder POV |
| Creative | 25 | volume, angle diversity, awareness coverage, performance, production system |
| Association | 15 | customer proof, creators, experts, influencers, PR, celebrity/partnerships |
| Distribution | 25 | channel mix, traffic quality, owned audience, reach expansion, retention |

Interpretation:
- 85-100: strong scaling foundation
- 70-84: good but with clear constraints
- 50-69: fragile growth; scale carefully
- below 50: fix fundamentals before adding spend

## Level Weighting

Different levels should care about different scorecards.

| Level | Highest-weight scores |
|---|---|
| 1 | Product, Creative, Copywriting, CRO basics |
| 2 | Creative, Offer/Funnel, Product, CRO |
| 3 | Creative, CRO, Offer/Funnel, Fulfillment/Cash, Product Development |
| 4 | Creative, Product Development, Offer/Funnel, Fulfillment/Cash, Branding/Association |
| 5 | Distribution, Brand/Association, Product Portfolio, Cash, Experimentation |

## Bottleneck Interpretation

If Product is low:
- do not scale spend
- diagnose demand, repeat, reviews, refunds, and product-market fit

If Creative is low:
- increase concept volume and awareness coverage
- build creator/founder/static testing pipeline

If Copywriting is low:
- mine customer language
- rebuild promise, proof, mechanism, objections, and CTA

If Offer/Funnel is low:
- redesign attraction offer, bundle, upsell, downsell, and repeat path

If CRO is low:
- fix mobile, proof, message match, checkout, speed, and landing page type

If Product Development is low:
- stop random SKU launches
- add stage gates and post-mortems

If Fulfillment/Cash is low:
- fix RTO, COD, inventory, contribution, and cash visibility before pushing spend
