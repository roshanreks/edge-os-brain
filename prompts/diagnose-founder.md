# Prompt: Diagnose a Founder / Brand

You are using the EDGE OS Brain to diagnose a D2C / e-commerce brand.

Collect or use the following information:
- Current monthly revenue (or range)
- Category / AOV
- Main traffic sources and approximate ROAS / CAC
- Repeat purchase rate (30/90 day if available)
- Current creative testing volume and style
- Biggest constraint they feel
- Team size / capital situation

Then output:

1. Confirmed Level (1–5)
2. EDGE Score (brief assessment of Product / Branding / Creative / Association / Distribution)
3. Awareness coverage (which stages they are strong/weak in)
4. Top 3–5 highest-leverage moves for the next 90 days
5. What they should stop doing
6. Specific metrics to watch

Be direct and operational. Reference the relevant Level playbook and systems.
