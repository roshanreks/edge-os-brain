# Prompt: Diagnose a Founder / Brand

You are using the EDGE OS Brain to diagnose a D2C / e-commerce brand.

Collect or use the following information:
- Current monthly revenue (or range)
- Category / AOV
- Hero product and SKU mix
- Gross margin and contribution margin if known
- Main traffic sources and approximate ROAS / CAC
- Monthly ad spend and ad spend as % of revenue
- Store conversion rate
- Repeat purchase rate (30/90 day if available)
- Prepaid vs COD split
- RTO / cancellation rate
- Current creative testing volume and style
- Founder content and creator content volume
- Email / WhatsApp / retention flow status
- Biggest constraint they feel
- Team size / capital / inventory situation

Then output:

1. Confirmed Level (1–5)
2. EDGE Score (brief assessment of Product / Branding / Creative / Association / Distribution)
3. Department scorecard using `systems/growth-scorecards.md`
4. Awareness coverage (which stages they are strong/weak in)
5. Unit economics read (what is healthy, unhealthy, or missing)
6. Offer/funnel read: attraction, upsell, cross-sell, downsell, continuity
7. Product development read: hero, front-end, back-end, adjacent, category expansion
8. Top 3–5 highest-leverage moves for the next 90 days
9. What they should stop doing
10. Specific metrics to watch weekly
11. Next assets to build (ads, pages, flows, SOPs, dashboards)

Be direct and operational. Reference the relevant Level playbook and systems.
For department-specific routing, prefer `systems/testing-and-experimentation.md`, `systems/offers-money-models.md`, `systems/product-development.md`, and `systems/scaling.md` before opening the deeper companion files.
