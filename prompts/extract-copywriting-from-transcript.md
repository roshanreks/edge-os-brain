# Prompt: Extract Copywriting Knowledge from Transcript

You are expanding the EDGE OS Brain with copywriting knowledge.

I will give you a transcript, note, or article about copywriting, advertising, offers, hooks, persuasion, or customer psychology.

Your job:

1. Summarize the core thesis in 3-5 sentences.
2. Extract every usable copywriting principle.
3. Map each principle to:
   - awareness stage
   - market sophistication stage if relevant
   - EDGE component
   - revenue Level 1-5
   - asset type: ad, PDP, landing page, email, WhatsApp, creator brief, founder content
4. Extract specific hook formulas, claims, proof structures, CTA ideas, and objection-handling ideas.
5. Turn the best ideas into reusable templates.
6. Identify where `systems/copywriting-engine.md` should be updated.
7. Flag weak, generic, or unsupported advice that should not enter the brain.

Output clean Markdown that can be placed in `/wiki/` first. Only recommend updates to `/systems/` when the insight is durable and repeatedly useful.
