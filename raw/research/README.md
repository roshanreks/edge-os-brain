# Raw Research

Store untouched research inputs here:
- reports
- article notes
- benchmark screenshots
- pasted source excerpts
- market research
- competitor teardown notes

Rules:
- Do not rewrite raw source files.
- Add source URL, date accessed, and short context when possible.
- Synthesized insights should move to `/wiki/` or the relevant `/systems/` file.

Suggested filename format:

`YYYY-MM-DD-source-topic.md`
