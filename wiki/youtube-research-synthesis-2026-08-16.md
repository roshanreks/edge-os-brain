## YouTube / Podcast Sources Reviewed

### Marketing Operators - Big-Swing vs. Incremental: Our Ecommerce CRO Testing Framework

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=auKUEvA_R8o

Useful ideas:
- CRO should use both big-swing and incremental testing.
- Big redesigns should carry forward previous learning rather than wiping the slate clean.
- After a big-swing win, decompose the winning page to find which sections drive value.
- RPU/RPS and AOV can matter more than CVR alone.
- Qualitative research from surveys, reviews, and customer feedback can create stronger test ideas than copying best practices.
- AI can help synthesize customer feedback, audit themes, and prioritize test opportunities.

### Marketing Operators - The Right Way to A/B Test Landing Pages on Meta Ads for Ecommerce

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj5d_YVNOC4

Useful ideas:
- Landing pages can be used as creative/destination diversity for Meta, not only as clean A/B tests.
- A page should match the ad angle, customer segment, and awareness stage.
- More landing pages can help learning and reach, but only if the team has a decision system and does not create chaos.
- Offer, destination, and message match should be considered together.

### Marketing Operators - New Product Launch Strategy, Net New Reach on Meta, and Big Partnership Swings

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NSDzXqkn7G8

Useful ideas:
- Launches need social proof quickly: creator videos, customer reactions, community, and early reviews.
- Partnership and podcast-style channels can create net-new reach.

### Marketing Operators - Go-To-Market Strategy: How 9-Figure Brands Plan, Execute, and Post-Mortem Product Launches

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeuL6I05S-g

Useful ideas:

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-BcsCvkC7uQ

Useful ideas:
- Landing pages should be built from reusable modules.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e4xsDBW_iSg

Useful ideas:

### Hormozi / Acquisition.com-Style Money Model Videos

Sources:
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HqmjCUSXkHE
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4rFOfpZEXzQ
- Official course menu: https://www.acquisition.com/training/money/menu-upsell

Useful ideas:
- Money models stack four offer roles: attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity.
- Attraction offers maximize first purchase or lead/customer conversion.
- Upsells increase cash collected after intent is proven.
- Downsells recover buyers who reject the premium path.
- Continuity creates repeat, subscription, refill, or membership value.
- E-commerce should adapt these ideas ethically and operationally: no fake urgency, no unprofitable bundles, no confusing checkout.

Installed into:
- `systems/offer-architecture-funnels.md`
- `systems/growth-scorecards.md`

## Written Sources Used

### Baymard - Product Page UX

Source: https://baymard.com/research/product-page

Useful ideas:
- PDP is a centerpiece of the e-commerce experience.
- Baymard's product-page research spans many guidelines and benchmarked sites.
- Product imagery, buy section, shipping/returns, variations, descriptions, reviews, auxiliary content, and cross-sells are all distinct UX areas.

### SplitBase - Product Page Highlights

Source: https://splitbase.com/blog/high-converting-product-page

Useful ideas:
- Product-page optimization should be research/test-led, not copied blindly.
- Reviews, product visuals, benefit explanations, how-to content, and objection handling are core.
- Upsells/cross-sells should be relevant and tested.

### SplitBase - 3 Types of Ecommerce Landing Pages

Source: https://splitbase.com/blog/landing-page-types

Useful ideas:
- The three common DTC landing page types are Hero, Quiz, and Advertorial.
- The page type should match funnel stage and buyer awareness.

### Sharma Brands / Nik Sharma - Landing Page Mistakes

Source: https://sharmabrands.com/blogs/newsletter/7-biggest-landing-page-mistakes

Useful ideas:
- Avoid dead clicks and too-forward CTAs.
- Explain next steps after purchase.
- Customer protection/returns/support clarity improves confidence.
- Benefits should be written as concrete customer outcomes, not generic value props.

### Shopify / Nik Sharma - Product Page Conversion

Source: https://www.shopify.com/blog/ask-nik-how-do-i-optimize-my-product-pages-for-high-conversions

Useful ideas:
- Optimize for mobile.
- Prioritize above-the-fold clarity.
- CRO is ongoing, not one project.
- Combine quantitative and qualitative tools to understand where and why visitors leave.

### CXL / VWO / Optimizely - A/B Testing Discipline

Sources:
- https://cxl.com/conversion-rate-optimization/how-to-run-ab-tests-by-peep-laja/
- https://vwo.com/ab-testing/
- https://www.optimizely.com/optimization-glossary/ab-testing

Useful ideas:
- A/B tests need enough sample size and representative duration.
- Tests should have a hypothesis and clear primary metric.
- Low-traffic brands should not over-trust inconclusive A/B tests.
