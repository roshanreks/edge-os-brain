## What A Money Model Is

A money model is a **sequence of offers** that gets more customers to spend more money, in less time, more times.

It is not a pricing trick. It is the commercial architecture around the product.

Hormozi's cash test: **make more profit on a customer than it costs to acquire them, fast enough that cash flow is not the ceiling** (he often uses a 30-day payback frame).

EDGE OS already said this in unit-economics language. Money models are how you *design* that outcome instead of hoping repeat purchase appears.

## The Only Four Offer Types

1. **Attraction** — turn strangers into customers
2. **Upsell** — get them to spend more immediately
3. **Downsell** — recover the no
4. **Continuity** — get them to spend on a rhythm

Build in that order. Start with one of each around the hero product. Do not invent 16 tactics on day one.

## The 16 Plays (Acquisition.com Taxonomy)

### Attraction
1. Win Your Money Back
2. Free Giveaways
3. Decoy Offers
4. Buy X Get Y
5. Pay Less Now
6. Free with Consumption

Notes from practitioner extracts:
- Win-your-money-back needs easy-to-track criteria and realistic results. Credit toward a bigger purchase is often smarter than cash out.
- Giveaways: everyone should feel like a partial winner. Draw in 3–7 days. Then sell a participation offer (often 10–30% of gross margin) to non-winners.
- Decoy exists so the premium path is the obvious adult choice.
- Buy X Get Y: make Y feel bigger than X. "Buy 1 get 2 free" often beats "66% off".
- Pay less now / more later pairs with a conditional guarantee.
- Talk as if they will take the better offer. Enthusiasm transfers.

### Upsell
7. Classic Upsell ("can't have X without Y")
8. Menu Upsell / A/B choice
9. Anchor Upsell
10. Rollover Upsell

Also taught in the book / recap videos:
- Unselling (remove what they don't need)
- Prescription upsell (personalized usage)
- Stacking upsells
- Warranties / "peace of mind" add-ons

Notes:
- First offer does not have to be the profit center. Upsells can repair margin — *if* they are relevant and do not spike RTO.
- Menu script: which do you want, not whether you want.
- Anchor: show the 5–10x premium first. Wait. Then offer the core.
- Rollover: credit old spend or even a competitor's unused value into a bigger offer, used now. Winback engine.

### Downsell
11. Payment Plans
12. Free Trials (or trial-with-conditions)
13. Feature Downsells

Notes:
- Reward paying in full with a bonus rather than punishing installments with junk fees.
- Feature downsell: remove guarantee, speed, gift, quantity, or service level. Customers often upsell themselves back.
- DIY vs do-it-with-you vs do-it-for-you is a service frame; in D2C it becomes trial / standard / ritual kit / concierge gifting.

### Continuity
14. Continuity Bonus Offers
15. Continuity Discounts
16. Waived Fee

Notes:
- Sell the bonus, not "please join our subscription".
- People will often pay more to avoid feeling trapped. Handle this with honesty and easy pause, not dark patterns.
- Waived fee: setup or delivery fee of 3–5x a period, waived if they commit to a term. If they cancel early, fee returns.
- Cancellation is a conversation: exit interview, save offer, then let them leave cleanly.

## How To Build One (Hormozi's Close)

1. Start with an attraction offer
2. Pick one upsell
3. Pick one downsell for the people who said no
4. Pick one continuity offer
5. Sell the same thing more ways before inventing more things

More ways to sell the hero beats more SKUs.

## India D2C Constraints That Change The Plays

| Hormozi default | India constraint | EDGE OS adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Card-on-file continuity | UPI / COD world | WhatsApp refill + saved UPI + easy pause |
| Unconditional money-back | COD + RTO | Conditional guarantee + prepaid bias |
| Payment plans | Credit card less universal | BNPL only on high AOV; prepaid bonus otherwise |
| Aggressive giveaways | Discount addiction + festive clutter | Prize + product education + tight window |
| Setup fees | Trust-sensitive buyers | Frame as delivery / QC / cold-chain, not punishment |

## Where This Sits In EDGE

- **Product:** the hero and the next-problem SKU
- **Branding:** named kits and rituals become brand codes
- **Creative:** attraction offers need Problem/Solution-aware ads; continuity needs Most-aware
- **Association:** creators should sell the system/kit, not a naked SKU
- **Distribution:** marketplace and q-comm may not allow the full money model — keep the complete model on owned D2C
