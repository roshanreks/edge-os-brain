# Level 2: 5 – 20 Lacs Monthly

**Primary Job:** Double down hard on what is already working and raise creative volume + early systems.

You have proof of concept. Now the job is to multiply the edge instead of looking for a new one every week.

## What This Level Feels Like
- Revenue is more consistent but still founder-dependent
- One or two creatives / offers are carrying most of the revenue
- CAC is starting to feel the pressure of competition
- Team is still small; founder is the bottleneck on creative and decisions
- Retention is visible but not yet systematized

## Key Metrics (Healthy Ranges)
- Monthly revenue: 5–20 Lacs
- Ad spend as % of revenue: 25–35%
- Creative testing volume: 20–50 new concepts per month
- 90-day repeat rate: 22–30%+ is strong signal
- Blended ROAS should be improving as you lean into winners
- Contribution margin after ads becoming clearer

## EDGE Focus at This Level
1. **Creative** — Go heavy on the winning angles. Test variations aggressively.
2. **Product** — Protect and improve the core product that is working. Resist random new SKUs.
3. **Branding** — Sharpen positioning and voice so creative stays consistent.
4. Start light Association (nano/micro influencers) if it multiplies the existing edge.

**Core rule from the original map:** You have found what is working → go heavy on creatives, test more funnels, make more of the edge.

## Creative & Content Recommendations
- Increase volume significantly
- Keep producing low-effort + founder content as the base
- Begin introducing higher-effort versions of proven concepts
- Expand into more Solution-Aware and Problem-Aware angles to grow the audience pool
- Build a simple creative testing cadence (weekly or bi-weekly batches)

## Ad Spend & Testing
- 25–35% of revenue on paid is typical
- Protect 15–25% of media budget for testing new concepts
- Graduate winners systematically into scaling campaigns
- Start watching frequency and creative fatigue closely

## Conversion / Store Priorities
- Improve mobile CVR (this is usually the biggest leak)
- Strengthen social proof and trust elements
- Test offer structures (bundles, free shipping thresholds, upsells)
- Make the PDP match the winning ad angles

## Fulfillment & Operations
- Start treating fulfillment reliability as part of the product experience
- Reduce RTO with better verification and descriptions
- Packaging can begin to support unboxing content

## Capital Allocation Priority
1. Scale proven winners (inventory + media)
2. Creative production & testing engine
3. Basic retention systems (flows that actually drive second purchase)
4. Light team / freelancers for creative and ops support

## Offer & Money Model (Hormozi @ L2)
The first purchase must create a designed second purchase.

Install:
- Offer matrix: starter / bundle / prepaid perk / guarantee
- Classic upsell: the next-problem complement
- Menu upsell: "1 or 2?"
- Feature downsell: trial size if they bounce on price
- WhatsApp refill reminder (continuity lite)

Score: upsell take rate, prepaid mix, 60/90-day repeat, contribution after gifts.

Deep system: `systems/offers-money-models.md`

## Testing @ L2
- 20–50 concepts/month in weekly batches
- Spend gate: roughly ₹1,500–5,000 per concept
- Variants of winners (70%) + new angles (30%)
- First real landing test: PDP vs a page that matches the winning problem angle
- Hide coupon box. Reviews + images + checkout first.

Deep system: `systems/testing-and-experimentation.md`

## Product Development @ L2
- Protect the hero
- Add only the complement the first product creates
- Build one honest kit, not leftover bundles

Deep system: `systems/product-development.md`

## How To Scale @ L2
Scale the winning *angle* and the offer matrix, not random SKUs or celebrity.

Raise spend only when creative refresh and inventory can take it.

Deep system: `systems/scaling.md`

## Common Traps
- Getting bored of the winning creative and abandoning it too early
- Launching too many new products instead of multiplying the edge
- Still running everything as one-off campaigns instead of systems
- Ignoring retention while chasing new customers
- Letting CAC rise without improving LTV
- Discounting the core instead of stacking bonuses

## 90-Day Action Checklist
- [ ] Document the top 3 converting angles and create 10+ variations of each
- [ ] Set a weekly creative production target and stick to it
- [ ] Build or improve post-purchase flows that drive second order
- [ ] Fix the top 3 mobile conversion leaks
- [ ] Track contribution margin per order, not just ROAS
- [ ] Identify the single biggest operational bottleneck and resolve it
- [ ] Decide which products/SKUs to de-prioritize

**Exit criteria to Level 3:** Consistent revenue in the upper half of the band, clear creative system, measurable improvement in repeat rate, and the ability to scale spend without immediate collapse in efficiency.
