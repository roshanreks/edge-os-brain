# Level 1: 0 – 5 Lacs Monthly

**Primary Job:** Find real Product-Market Fit and extract the first clear EDGE.

This is the validation stage. Most brands that fail later do so because they never properly completed this level.

## What This Level Feels Like
- Founder is still heavily involved in everything
- Revenue is inconsistent
- Heavy reliance on a small number of creatives or one traffic source
- Unit economics are often negative or barely break-even on first order
- The biggest question is still “Does this product actually work?”

## Key Metrics (Healthy Ranges)
- Monthly revenue: 0–5 Lacs
- Ad spend as % of revenue: 30–50% (acceptable while validating)
- Creative testing volume: 8–20 new concepts per month
- Repeat purchase rate (90-day): Aim for 20%+ as signal of real PMF
- Store conversion rate: Anything above 1.5% is usable; focus more on offer clarity than perfection
- Primary goal: Prove that someone will buy *and* come back

## EDGE Focus at This Level
Priority order:
1. **Product** — Is there genuine demand and early retention?
2. **Creative** — Find 1–3 angles that convert cold traffic
3. **Branding** — Basic positioning and voice (enough to be consistent)
4. Association & Distribution are secondary until product and creative show signal

**Rule:** Once something is working (a creative, an offer, a specific customer type), extract that edge and deliberately make more of it.

## Creative & Content Recommendations
- Heavy on low-effort + founder content
- Simple UGC, talking-head founder videos, static carousels, customer screenshots
- Test problem-aware and product-aware angles primarily
- Do not over-invest in high-production yet

## Ad Spend & Testing
- 30–50% of revenue on paid media is normal
- Keep 20–30% of media budget for pure testing
- Kill losers fast; scale only clear winners
- Focus on Meta first (highest learning speed at this stage)

## Conversion / Store Priorities
- Mobile-first (most traffic is mobile)
- Clear offer + social proof above the fold
- Fast page speed
- Simple checkout with UPI + COD options
- Do not chase 3%+ CVR yet — get the offer and product right first

## Fulfillment & Operations
- Reliability > sophistication
- Reduce RTO with basic COD verification and clear product descriptions
- Packaging should be clean and on-brand but does not need to be a content asset yet

## Capital Allocation Priority
1. Inventory for proven winners only
2. Creative testing
3. Basic retention (email/WhatsApp)
4. Everything else secondary

## Offer & Money Model (Hormozi @ L1)
One hero. One attraction offer. No stack of upsells.

Install:
- A named starter: Buy X Get Y, or trial + full size, or simple kit
- A specific guarantee (conditional is safer with COD)
- UPI perk rather than deeper and deeper discounts
- Nothing after purchase except "how to use" + review ask

Do not:
- Build a 4-step funnel
- Win-your-money-back on unverified COD
- Launch a subscription you cannot operate

Score weekly: is the offer clearer than last month, and did anyone repurchase?

Deep system: `systems/offers-money-models.md`

## Testing @ L1
- 8–20 concepts. Low-effort. Founder on camera.
- Spend gate: roughly ₹500–2,000 per concept
- No button-color A/B tests. Fix the obvious: ATF, images, reviews, checkout, speed
- Watch sessions (Clarity) before guessing
- Kill ads that get clicks and no ATC

Deep system: `systems/testing-and-experimentation.md`

## Product Development @ L1
- Improve the hero from customer language
- One size / flavour / shade that actually works beats a catalogue
- Write the one-page product brief before any new SKU

Deep system: `systems/product-development.md`

## How To Scale @ L1
Do not scale spend. Scale *learning*: more conversations, more concepts, clearer offer.

Exit to L2 is proof, not a festival spike.

Deep system: `systems/scaling.md`

## Common Traps
- Scaling ad spend before repeat rate shows real PMF
- Chasing too many products or SKUs
- Over-designing the store instead of testing offers
- Ignoring founder-led content
- Treating every creative as permanent instead of temporary tests
- Copying a Hormozi webinar funnel onto a ₹499 SKU

## 90-Day Action Checklist
- [ ] Write a clear one-page customer + problem statement
- [ ] Run at least 15–20 distinct creative concepts
- [ ] Track 30/60/90-day repeat rate religiously
- [ ] Identify the single strongest converting angle and double down
- [ ] Fix the biggest mobile checkout friction points
- [ ] Set up basic post-purchase WhatsApp/email flow
- [ ] Decide what to stop selling or stop promoting

**Exit criteria to Level 2:** Consistent monthly revenue near the top of the band + clear evidence of what is working (product + creative edge) + early positive unit economics signals.
